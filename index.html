Can I put a granny flat here?

Enter an Auckland property address. This tool pulls zoning and constraint layers straight from Auckland Council's public GIS services to give you a first-pass read on minor dwelling / second dwelling feasibility.
Start typing — matching Auckland addresses appear below automatically. Can't find it? Use "Maps ↗" to confirm the address on Google Maps first.
Not a substitute for a LIM or planner. This screening tool checks Auckland Council's public map layers against the property's actual parcel boundary where that boundary can be found, plus the national granny-flat standard (NES-DMRU, in force since 15 January 2026) for setback and floor-area rules. It doesn't check every remaining district plan rule (e.g. height-in-relation-to-boundary for anything larger than a permitted DMRU, parking, or overlapping precinct rules), map data can lag real-world changes, and there's no public layer here for Watercare's drinking-water main locations (only wastewater and stormwater are published) — a Watercare as-built request or Before U Dig NZ enquiry is still needed for that. Before committing, order a LIM, check the Auckland Unitary Plan rules for the specific zone and any precincts on the property, and consider a pre-application meeting with Auckland Council.
